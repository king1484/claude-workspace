## Time model (shared by everything)
`H` = hours since sunrise, 0..100 (wraps). `elev = sin(2*PI*H/100)`: H=0 sunrise (sun on the horizon, EAST/left), H=25 noon (overhead), H=50 sunset (WEST/right), H=75 midnight, H=100 sunrise again. Sun travels left -> right across the sky. Moon/stars own the night. A chapter maps its local time `lt` to `H` however its story needs (it may jump, e.g. hook is at H=25, chapter 3 starts at H=0 with a rewind gag).

### tod.js  (global `TOD`) — time-of-day world
- `TOD.pal(H, o)` -> palette `{skyTop, skyMid, skyHor, sunCol, light, shade, ground, road, walk, bld:[far,mid,near], rim, haze, win(alpha of lit windows), star(alpha), night(0..1), heat(0..1)}` (css strings / numbers); smooth through: pre-dawn blue, sunrise gold/pink, bright morning, white-hot noon, golden evening, red sunset, dusk violet, deep night.
- `TOD.drawWorld(g, cam, H, o)` — full background for a street scene: sky, sun/moon, stars (when dark), clouds, 3 parallax city layers (far/mid/near) with sun-driven side lighting + lit windows at night, ground/road/sidewalk. `cam = {x, y, z, r}` exactly like video 1 (`world.js` withLayer/project semantics: character stands on the sidewalk at y = `SIDEWALK_Y` (820) around x = 900). `o = {scene:'city'|'desert'|'farm'|'coast', heat:0..1, frost:0..1, wilt:0..1 (plants), cloud:0..1, t:seconds, sunX?:override}`.
- `TOD.drawForeground(g, cam, H, o)` — near-camera depth elements (lamp posts, hedges, rocks/cactus/crops depending on scene), to draw AFTER characters.
- `TOD.drawCastShadow(g, H, x, y, s, drawFn)` — long sun-driven character shadow on the ground (length ~ 1/tan(elev), clamped, direction away from the sun, shortest at noon, none at night). `drawFn(g)` draws the figure in a flat shadow palette (use `TOD.PAL_SHADOW` with `drawWalker`).
- `TOD.sunScreen(H, cam)` -> `{x, y, r, visible}`; `TOD.moonScreen(H, cam)`.
- Effects (all pure functions of inputs): `TOD.heatWarp(g, y0, y1, amt, t)` (shimmering wavy distortion of a horizontal band via a scratch canvas), `TOD.heatGlare(g, amt, t)` (white-hot bloom + wobbling sun rays), `TOD.frostOverlay(g, amt, t)` (white crystal vignette on frame edges + sparkles), `TOD.frostGround(g, cam, amt)`, `TOD.stars(g, H, alpha, o)` (rotating starfield + milky way band; rotation ∝ H so it barely moves), `TOD.grade(g, H, o)` (colour grade overlay for noon-bleach / night-blue / golden), `TOD.rays(g, x, y, k, t)`.
- `TOD.hud(g, H, o)` — the persistent progress clock in the top-left corner: round sun/moon dial whose hand sweeps with H, plus "HOUR nn / 100" in chunky rounded white text with dark outline, and a thin 0..100 progress bar with a sun marker. `o = {alpha, pop:0..1 (scale-in), pulse}`. Keep it small (<= 300x110 px) and tasteful.
- Performance: heavy static art (skylines, stars, clouds, textures) cached in offscreen canvases; per-frame work = a few hundred fills. **Must be <= ~25 ms** for a plain `drawWorld`.

### mascot.js (global `MASCOT`) — the expressive host
Wraps rigs.js. `MASCOT.draw(g, st)`, `st = {x, y, s, facing, pose, t, expr, props, pal, alpha, shadow:false}`. Needs: poses `stand, walk(speed), run, sit (on ground / bench), lie (sleeping), kneel, slump, point(dir), shield (arm over eyes), fanSelf, hugSelf (cold), wipeBrow, throwArms, facepalm, sprawl, lookUp, thumbsUp`; expression channels (head has NO brows/mouth, so expression = eye shapes + body language + FX): eyes `normal|wide|squint|droop|closed|happy|spiral|dead`, `look` direction, blink (deterministic), `sweat` (drops that run down the head), `shiver` (jitter + chattering), `steam`, `sleepZ`, `sparkle`; props `umbrella, sunglasses, scarf, blanket, fan, waterBottle, plate/food, flashlight, towel on head, mug`. Frame-pure.

### globe.js (global `GLOBE`) — planet renderer
`GLOBE.draw(g, cx, cy, R, o)`: stylised orthographic Earth (recognisable continents from hand-made lon/lat polygons -> cached heightfield), `o = {lon, lat (tilt), sunLon, sunLat (day/night terminator), sea:-1..1 (sea-level redistribution: >0 drains equatorial seas, exposing seafloor, and floods polar lowlands; <0 reverse), oblate:0..1 (exaggerated equatorial bulge for the diagram), spinArrow:0..1, clouds:0..1, atmo:true, nightLights:true, tNow}`. Also `GLOBE.bulgeDiagram(g, cx, cy, R, o)` (squashed-ball cross-section with water layer showing the bulge and the water sliding to the poles) and `GLOBE.dayNightArrows` helper for the weather chapter if useful. Must be fast (<= 12 ms) — cache the heightfield, render the sphere by per-pixel sampling into a reused ImageData at modest resolution then upscale if needed.
