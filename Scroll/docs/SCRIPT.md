# SCROLL — script and shot list (v1, for approval)

Silent animated short. No narration, no dialogue. Runtime target **3:50** (hard limit under 5:00). Channel: new, separate from What If Labs.

**Logline.** A man promises himself one thing tonight, a drawing, and loses the whole night to a feed with no bottom, until he finally builds one.

**Lesson (shown, never narrated):** *The feed has no bottom. Build one.* Concretely: decide the last swipe before the first, and make the end physical (charger and phone across the room).

**Open question the film answers:** "Where does the feed end?" Asked at 0:05, answered at 2:20 (the phone reaches a floor) and restated at 3:35.

## Look and colour rules (the story is carried by colour)

- The world is **grey**. Target mean saturation below 25% until 2:28.
- **Cold blue** is the feed (phone glow, notification dots, falling icons). The only light in the room is the phone.
- **Warm amber and the red hoodie** are the real world. The hoodie starts grey, and turns red from the hem upward during the drawing at 2:36–2:48. Nothing else in the film is red.
- His **shadow** is a separate grey character on the wall: it is the part of him that wanted to rest. It sleeps at 0:05 and 1:27, and wakes at 2:28.
- No real app names, logos or interfaces. All feed content is original vector gags.
- Character: faceless black head, two white eyes, hoodie, black trousers (same mascot as What If Labs, grey hoodie to start).
- On-screen text: at most 8 words at a time. Only these appear: the cards below, clock times, one screen-time number, one sticky note.

## Pacing targets (checked after the animatic with `tools/analyze_reference.py`)

- Mean saturation under 25% until 2:28; hoodie red only after 2:36.
- Zero spoken words. Text never above 8 words.
