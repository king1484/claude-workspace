# SCROLL — sound design (v1)

Piano, room tone and foley only. No whooshes, no stock risers, no synth pads (one very low, quiet drone is allowed under the hook and payoff). The mix is quiet and wide in dynamics on purpose: the references we measured vary by about 4 LU (1 MINUTE); our previous video varied by 0.4 LU and felt flat.

## Musical idea: the third that never arrives

- Key centre **D**, tempo **64 BPM**, solo felt-soft piano.
- Core motif: **D – A – G – rest**, an open fifth with no third. It sounds neither happy nor sad. It hangs.
- The note **F♯** (the major third that would resolve it to D major) is withheld for the whole film. Notification pings are tuned to D, A and G, so even the sound effects avoid it.
- **F♯ arrives only at 2:20**, the moment the coin lands. From then on the motif resolves and the harmony is major.
- If the viewer hears nothing else, they hear "something finally landed".

## Mix targets

- Integrated loudness about **-16 LUFS**; true peak no higher than **-1.5 dBTP**.
- Short-term loudness by section: Hook -24 to -20, Setup -24, Pull -17 to -15 (loudest), Shadow -22, Low point -28, Silence under -40 for 3 s, Turn -22, Colour -20, Payoff -20, Coda -26 and fading.
- Spread between the quietest and loudest 10 s bucket of at least **6 LU**.
- Piano never above -16 dBFS peak. Foley sits 6–12 dB under the piano, audible on small speakers but never competing. Only designed hits (the coin landing at 2:20) may stand above the piano.
- Check on speakers, phone and headphones, and at low volume (the clock must still be audible).

## Sourcing

- **Piano.** A real sampled piano will sound much better than anything I can synthesize. Free sampled pianos are online, but I cannot download them from this environment: GitHub and FreePats return 403. Options, in order of preference:
  1. You download a free sampled piano (for example the CC-BY *Salamander Grand Piano* or a CC0 FreePats upright), commit it to this repo, and I render the score with it.
  2. You record or supply your own piano or a track you have rights to.
  3. Fallback: I synthesize a physically modelled felt piano in code. Usable, quieter and simpler, not realistic.
- **Foley and SFX.** Freesound is reachable but downloads need an account or API key. Options: you give me a Freesound API key as an environment secret and I pull CC0 sounds only, or I synthesize foley in code (the repo already has `sfx_full.mjs`). I'd start with the synthesized set, since every cue is tuned to the key.
- **Licences.** Anything used from outside is logged with source URL and licence in `Scroll/docs/CREDITS.md`. CC-BY needs credit in the description.
