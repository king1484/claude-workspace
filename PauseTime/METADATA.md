## Description
```
One snap and the whole world stops. Sounds perfect... until you realise what happens to light, sound and air.

What would YOU do with paused time? Tell me in the comments.

Science note: this is a thought experiment. If time stopped for everything except you, light would stop too, so nothing would reach your eyes; and the air around you would stay put instead of flowing out of your way.
```
